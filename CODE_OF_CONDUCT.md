# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Expected:

- Being kind and respectful, including to people you disagree with.
- Giving and taking constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@Hakam-aldeen-Kh](https://github.com/Hakam-aldeen-Kh), through the contact
details on their GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's
privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban a
participant, as set out in the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
